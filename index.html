    Division Words

    I can name the dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder.

    Every division problem has parts with special names. The dividend is the number being divided. The divisor is the number you divide by. The quotient is the answer. The remainder is what is left over. Drag each word under the right number.

    Long Division Coach

    I can divide up to a five-digit number by a two-digit number using the standard algorithm.

    Long division repeats four moves: Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down. A trick to remember them is "Does McDonald's Sell Burgers?" The coach will tell you each move. Drag the right number into the glowing box. Use the multiples list to help you divide.

    Training Room 3

    Remainders as Fractions

    I can write a remainder as a fraction of the divisor.

    A remainder is part of one more group. You can write it as a fraction. The remainder becomes the numerator on top. The divisor becomes the denominator on the bottom. So 100 ÷ 7 = 14 R 2 can be written as 14 2/7. Drag the matching answer to each problem.

    Training Room 4

    Estimate and Check

    I can estimate a quotient and check my answer with multiplication.

    Before you divide, estimate. Round the divisor to the nearest ten. Then change the dividend to a nearby number that divides easily. For 6,318 ÷ 79, think 6,400 ÷ 80 = 80. After you divide, check: quotient × divisor + remainder should equal the dividend. Drag the best estimate to each problem.
